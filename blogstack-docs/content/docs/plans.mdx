All plans include SEO checks, analytics, SDK and editable components. Allowances are shared across the billing account. People are unique across that account's blogs; active invitations reserve a seat. There is no per-blog charge on Studio or Growth.

Each blog supports 1,000 posts and each image up to 10 MiB. Uploads reserve storage before a signed URL is issued. AI and external checks reserve allowance atomically before work starts. No automatic overage charges apply.

## Trial and payments

The Studio trial lasts 14 days from the first owned blog, requires no card and includes 100 AI credits. Joining a client's project does not start your own account's trial.

The account owner selects monthly or annual billing. Taxes are calculated at checkout. Polar handles checkout and payment details. Paid access follows confirmed payment service dates, never an unpaid renewal date.

Upgrades within the same billing interval invoice the prorated difference and update access after payment. Downgrades and interval changes take effect at renewal; reduce usage to the destination plan first. The destination's storage, people and blog ceilings are reserved while a downgrade is pending.

Cancel through Payments & cancellation. Editing continues to the end of confirmed coverage. Refund requests use the support process; a full refund or provider revocation may shorten paid access.

## Expiry and exports

Editing stops at expiry. Public API/media delivery continues for 30 days. Content and media exports remain available until deletion at 90 days, with advance email warnings.

Previously downloaded or customer-built content cannot be withdrawn by pausing BlogStack delivery. Export JSON includes live articles, private drafts, metadata, tags and authenticated media download paths. Download media before the retention deadline.
